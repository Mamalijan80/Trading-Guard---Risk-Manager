**Multi-prop-firm principle:** The logic is **not** hard-wired to FTMO. **FTMO is merely the default profile** (example: EUR 20,000 account, daily 5 % / max 10 %, reset 00:00 CE(S)T). ## Addition to the checklist — items 16–21 (new since v0.37)

16. ✅ **Master deadlock closed** *(v0.42/0.43/0.45, built, untested)* — only the **confirmed** master instance (`RG_MASTER`, hysteresis ≥ 2 cycles) does enforcement, close evaluation and cockpit writes. The old deinit release **deleted** `RG_MASTER`; `GlobalVariableSetOnCondition` cannot create a **missing** variable → after removing or re-attaching the master chart, a master election was **never** possible again. Consequence: enforcement (R4/R4b/R6/R7/R22 …), close evaluation (losing streak, cooldown, daily budget) and cockpit updates stood still — and did so **invisibly**, because the panel kept running normally on every chart. Fix: release via `GlobalVariableSet(GV_MASTER,0.0)` instead of `Del` in `OnDeinit` (the slot remains → immediate takeover); `ClaimMaster()` creates a missing slot up front via `GlobalVariableTemp` and then still claims it atomically via `SetOnCondition`; alarm (`Notify` + `PROTECT_OFF` journal) after **30 s without a master**. v0.43 only raises the alarm when **truly nobody** is master (slot free or heartbeat > 10 s old) — a passive instance is the normal case and must not warn permanently; v0.45 measures those 30 s time-based instead of in cycles (otherwise the deadline depended on `InpTimerSeconds`) and defuses the latch when transitioning into the master state.
    - **Testing needed (#14), not yet played through:** close the master chart → another instance takes over (≤ 10 s stale threshold) · close the **last** chart and re-attach the EA → the master election succeeds (that was the deadlock) · F3/recompile · start two instances at exactly the same time → exactly **one** becomes master · move the PC clock forwards and backwards (stale detection is bidirectional) · actually trigger the 30 s alarm and see it in the journal.
    - ⚠ **Diagnostic note for the forward test:** a deadlock that has occurred leaves no error message but **missing entries**. Actively cross-check: do `CLOSE` lines still arrive in the journal after every close, and does `mamal_cockpit.json` not age (the server reports `ageMs`)?

17. ✅ **Challenge / day base automatic (zero-config)** *(v0.38, built, untested)* — `DepositBase()` reads the first balance booking (`OrderType()==6`) from the history as the account size; a **plausibility clamp** discards a "deposit" **< 10 % of the current balance** (a fee refund with a filtered history tab would otherwise have made R4b ineffective: base ≪ balance → drawdown clamps to 0). On first start in the middle of the day, the day base is reconstructed via `ReconstructedBalanceAt(ServerDayStart())` and conservatively raised against balance/equity with `MathMax`; the same reconstruction applies on a late day or week roll. The derived base is persisted (`RG_INIT_BAL` + lockstate field 9) and protected against being lowered. `InpInitialBalance`/`InpDayStartBase` are therefore only an **optional override**; the same 10 % guard additionally catches a decimal separator typo in the override. "BASIS UNSICHER" (base uncertain) is practically abolished in normal operation.
    - **Fail-closed remains in place**, but only for the one genuine case: `g_initialBalance<=0` (no broker handshake at startup) → `RG_BASE_WARN=1`, status "BASIS UNSICHER" (base uncertain), every entry blocked. **Self-healing in the master cycle** as soon as `AccountBalance()>0` — with identical derivation and identical clamp.
    - **Documented limitation, not fixed:** `DepositBase()` and `ReconstructedBalanceAt()` only see the range **loaded in the account history tab** (the known `OrdersHistoryTotal` weakness). With a filtered tab, **too little** is corrected, never too much; `MathMax` prevents any deterioration relative to the actual state. **Testing needed (#14):** first start with the tab filter "last month" vs. "entire history" · account with an additional deposit · account with a withdrawal · account without a visible balance booking (→ fallback to the current balance) · override with a wrong decimal separator.
    - **Multi-account:** the EA writes a signpost `mamal_files_<login>.txt` per login into the Common folder; from it the cockpit server offers the account switcher (`?acct=…`, digits only → no path injection). Before the forward test, check that no data of the previous account is shown on a **different** account (`readState` discards a deviating `account`).

18. ✅ **Manual trades visible without distorting the rules** *(v0.44/0.45, built, untested)* — trades outside the tool (manual, mobile, foreign EAs) are written as their own journal event **`CLOSE_MAN`** and shown in the dashboard as **"MANUELL"** (manual) (orange row, ✋ in the calendar, its own statistics tile, its own column in the weekly report). They count towards the **net** (real money) but trigger **no rule**: no losing streak, no cooldown, no R3 daily budget. A day with manual trades **interrupts the discipline streak** ("sauberer Tag" / clean day = no block **and** nothing bypassing the panel).
    - **Important distinction for the prop firm view:** "no rule" does **not** mean "no protection". The prop-firm-critical limits R4 (daily), R4b (max loss) and R18 (weekly) compute against **equity vs. base** — a manual loss lowers the equity and is therefore captured by them **in full**. Only the behaviour-related rules (losing streak/cooldown) and the tool's own risk budget R3, which deliberately counts only trades with `Magic==InpMagic`, are excluded.
    - **Double counting closed (the actual fix):** `RGP_` markers are now pruned only after **6 h** (previously immediately along with the close floor → the ticket fallback no longer found any markers and evaluated everything a second time: duplicate `CLOSE` lines, duplicate losing streak, duplicate net in the calendar). In addition a **shared `RGM_` marker** (pruned after 2 days) sets both paths against each other: a trade can never run into the net as `CLOSE` **and** as `CLOSE_MAN` at the same time, not even with magic divergence or a scope change. `ResolveManualHistory()` aggregates over a **wider** window (day start − 7 days) than it reports, so that a partial close from yesterday does not appear as a partial amount; an implausible time base (`dayStart<=0` or in the future) → no action.
    - **Testing needed (#14):** open and close a manual trade on the phone → exactly **one** `CLOSE_MAN` line · partial close across midnight → **one** aggregated net · panel trade with a deviating magic → `CLOSE`, **not** additionally `CLOSE_MAN` · EA restart between open and close · marker not persistable (GV limit) → journal notice instead of a silent blank · reconcile the calendar net against the broker's account statement.

19. 🟡 **Verify panel close buttons per firm** *(v0.40, open — the second item next to R22)* — four panel buttons: `FULL CLOSE` / `CHART CLOSE` / `FULL 50%` / `CHART 50%`. Reach is **account-wide including foreign magics** (a deliberate user decision), `CHART …` filters on the chart symbol, `50 %` halves every position (rounded to the lot step, FP epsilon against `0.06*0.5/0.01 = 2.999…`, skip if the half **or** the remainder is below the minimum lot). Pending orders remain untouched. No `MarkEaClosed` → losses count normally towards the streak (no loss laundering). The buttons run **even during a lock and cooldown** — risk reduction is never blocked. `InpMagic<=0` is corrected to `990201` at startup.
    - **Why this is a compliance item of its own:** R22 explicitly leaves foreign EA magics untouched (see item 11). The close buttons do **not** — on request they also close positions of other EAs on the same account. The difference to R22: this is **user-initiated** (one click, not an automatic enforcement action) and journalled (`PANEL_CLOSE`). Even so, before a funded account it has to be clarified per firm whether an account-wide flatten button collides with order frequency / consistency rules — analogous to the questions under item 13.
    - **Testing needed (#14):** 50 % on 0.03 lots (odd) · 50 % on the minimum lot (→ unchanged, counter `skipped`) · 50 % on a panel trade → the **remaining ticket** is re-registered and evaluated correctly later · `FULL CLOSE` with an active day lock · trade context busy · close fail and requote case.

20. ⬜ **Secure and test the cockpit command channel (`mamal_cmd.txt`)** *(v0.39, open)* — the dashboard button "Tag beenden" (end day) makes the Node server write `mamal_cmd.txt` into the Files folder of the selected account; the EA reads, **deletes** and processes the file in the master cycle. The lock is **strictly tighten-only**: `MathMax` against a possibly already longer lock, effective only if it actually **extends** it, followed immediately by `GlobalVariablesFlush()` + `WriteLockstate()` (the cmd file is already deleted at that point — a crash in the 1 s window must not lose the voluntary lock). Server side: `acct` is mandatory, origin check against localhost. A file that cannot be deleted (read-only / AV lock) → a one-off notice instead of per-second spam.
    - **Honest classification:** the origin check only bites **if** an `Origin` header is set (curl/CLI get through), and any local process can write the file directly anyway. That is acceptable because the channel can **only tighten** — the damage case is an unwanted self-lock, not a loosening. Exactly this property must be **proven** in the test, not assumed.
    - **Testing needed (#14):** `endday` with an already running longer lock → **no** shortening, **no** second journal event · double click · manipulated cmd file with foreign text → ignored · file write-protected · command for account A while account B is open in the dashboard.

21. ⬜ **Confirm `SELECT_BY_TICKET` / ticket registry empirically** *(v0.40, open — the most important untested mechanism)* — close detection was made independent of magic and of the history tab: tickets opened from the panel are registered as `RGOPN_<ticket>`, `ResolveByTicketRegistry()` resolves them via `OrderSelect(..., SELECT_BY_TICKET)`, independently of the history tab's period filter and of the `GV_LAST_CLOSE` floor, and aggregates **all legs of the same position** together (otherwise a winning leg could be evaluated first and reset the losing streak despite a net loss).
    - **Why this stays open:** the entire construction rests on the assumption that `SELECT_BY_TICKET` resolves a ticket even when it is **not visible** in the history tab. In MQL4 this is **not guaranteed** and depends on broker/terminal. The code already takes this into account (registry diagnostics in the journal: "N vorgemerkt, M NICHT auflösbar" / N pre-booked, M NOT resolvable; unresolvable keys are **kept**, not discarded; `HistoryVisibilityWatch()` locks fail-closed if a closed ticket stays invisible) — but **it is not proven**.
    - **Gate:** before funded operation it must be measured at the target firm's broker: close a trade → switch the history tab to "today" / "last week" / "entire history" → in **every** setting exactly one `CLOSE` line with the correct net appears; registry diagnostics report 0 unresolvable tickets; check again after the 4-week GV expiry and after a terminal restart. If that fails, the losing-streak / daily-budget evaluation is not reliable — the fail-closed watchdog does bite then, but the tool is not challenge-capable that way.

## Addition to the checklist — items 22–28 (new since v0.45)

22. ✅ **RISK FREE — SL to break-even** *(v0.46, built, untested)* — `PanelBreakEven()` moves the SL to `OrderOpenPrice()` plus `InpBreakEvenBufferPts` (in points, negative values are clamped). Break-even is deliberately **exactly the entry price**: MT4 opens a BUY at the ask and closes at the bid (SELL the other way round), so the spread is already contained in the entry; the buffer additionally covers the commission. An SL is **never** loosened — positions already at or beyond break-even are skipped; it is only moved if the current price is genuinely in profit, and `MODE_STOPLEVEL` is respected (otherwise the server rejects the modify with err 130). Every change and every failure goes into the audit trail as its own journal event **`BREAKEVEN`** with the ticket.
    - **Compliance classification:** unlike the close buttons (item 19) and R22 (item 13), RISK FREE **does not extend the reach**. Only positions that satisfy `InScope()` **or** are registered as a panel ticket (`RGOPN_<ticket>`) are touched — foreign EA magics remain untouched, because their logic may depend on their own SL. It is pure risk reduction and therefore runs **even during a lock and cooldown**. No new firm-specific check item.
    - ⚠ **Make the reach precise:** the panel button calls `PanelBreakEven(true)` — it acts **only on the chart symbol**, not account-wide. Anyone with open positions on several symbols secures only one of them with a click. The label does not say so; either relabel it or add a second, account-wide button.

23. ✅ **Weekly risk mandatory** *(v0.49–v0.51, built, untested)* — instead of `[-]`/`[+]` there is an input field (`OBJ_EDIT`) with a "SETZEN" (set) button; Enter in the field confirms as well. The decimal **comma** is converted, because MQL4 would otherwise read "0,25" as 0 and silently discard the input; the field text is only rewritten when the valid value changes — otherwise `DrawPanel` (2×/s) would overwrite the ongoing input. Valid is `0 < v ≤ 1.0` (hard cap, plus `InpRiskMaxPct`). New is the **obligation** (`InpRequireWeeklyRisk`, default on): without a defined weekly risk, `DoEntry` blocks every entry (journal `BLOCKED` "Wochen-Risiko nicht festgelegt" / weekly risk not set), the panel shows "RISIKO FESTLEGEN" (set risk) and greys out BUY/SELL.
    - **Semantics:** the **first** choice of a week takes effect immediately — it also sets `RG_EFF_RISK`, otherwise the daily tighten-only layer would push it straight back down. **Every** later change in the same week — including **lowering** it — is only pre-booked for the following week (`RG_WEEK_RISK_NXT`). This is deliberately not tighten-only but "decided once, then bound for the week".
    - **Clarification:** the pre-booking does not take effect "from Monday" but at the **week anchor** from `WeekIdx()` — default **Sunday 00:00 server time**, shiftable via `InpWeekStartDay`/`InpDayResetHour`. Anyone setting a different week boundary for a prop firm (item 13) shifts this date too.
    - **Limitation:** the gate sits in `DoEntry` and therefore acts **only on panel trades**. A position opened manually in the terminal comes into existence despite the missing weekly decision — only R22 works against that. Likewise the obligation only applies with `InpRiskChooser=true`.

24. ✅ **Behaviour tracking, screenshots, candle thirds** *(v0.47/v0.52, built, untested)* — `TrackSLTP()` remembers the last seen SL/TP per ticket (`RGSLS_`/`RGTPS_`) and writes every move as journal event **`SLTP_MOVE`** with a plain-text verdict: SL further away = "Risiko ERHOEHT (nachteilhaft)" (risk INCREASED, disadvantageous), SL closer = "Risiko gesenkt (vorteilhaft)" (risk reduced, advantageous) including break-even detection, SL removed = "Risiko unbegrenzt" (risk unlimited), TP closer = "Gewinn abgekuerzt" (profit cut short), TP further = "Ziel vergroessert" (target enlarged). Counting does **not** happen during a running user modify series (`modifyQuiet`) — otherwise every intermediate step of the drag would count as a separate move; what is evaluated is the result after releasing. For every action an image `Mamal_<ticket>_<open|close|slmove|tpmove>_<epoch>.png` is created (`InpShotWidth`/`InpShotHeight`), cleaned up according to `InpShotKeepDays` with a cap of 200 deletions per run (no long I/O block). In addition the EA writes the **candle third** of the entry (`K1/3`…`K3/3`) into the `OPEN` tag; the cockpit computes the net per third from it.
    - **The evidentiary value of the images is limited:** `TrackSLTP()` and the close evaluation run **only on the master instance**, and `ChartScreenShot` photographs that instance's chart. An `slmove`, `tpmove` or `close` image therefore shows the **master chart**, not necessarily the symbol of the affected ticket. Only the `open` image is created on the chart that was clicked and reliably matches the trade.
    - **The candle third is chart-relative:** `CandleThird()` computes against `PeriodSeconds()` of the chart on which the entry was triggered. The same entry is "late" on M1 and "early" on H1. The statement "early/middle/late" is only comparable within a constantly held trading timeframe — that belongs written onto the evaluation in the dashboard.
    - **Watch the side effects in the forward test:** two additional GlobalVariables per open ticket (cleaned up on close) and one PNG file per action in `MQL4/Files`. Both are capped, but the GV limit, disk space and OneDrive/AV scans on the same path have to be checked.

25. 🟡 **Trade file in the cockpit** *(v0.62, built, with a known finding)* — `/trades` (list), `/trade?ticket=` (file), `/shot/<file>` (image) and the page `trade.html`. Per trade: planned risk (Risk% × balance of **the same** `OPEN` line, journal columns 13 and 3), number of moves split into riskier/safer, candle third, an automatically worded plain-text summary, the screenshots in chronological order with full-screen zoom and the chronicle of the moves with verdicts; for closed trades additionally the comparison planned risk ↔ result ("Der Verlust liegt 3,6× über dem geplanten Risiko — Ursache prüfen" / the loss is 3.6× the planned risk — check the cause). Access via the 📁 button in the dashboard header and via clickable journal rows with a ticket. The file name is checked strictly server-side against `^Mamal_\d+_[a-z0-9]+_\d+\.png$` — no other path is readable via `/shot/`.
    - ⚠ **Finding, to be fixed before the forward test:** the trade file only finds a **result** if the `CLOSE` line carries a ticket. In `Cycle()`, however, `ResolveHistory()` runs **before** `ResolveByTicketRegistry()`, and the group path writes its `CLOSE` lines with **ticket 0** (`ApplyResult(...,0,...)`); the ticket registry then skips the position with "Gruppen-Pfad war schneller" (the group path was faster). In the normal case — a closed trade visible in the history tab — the file therefore says "Position ist noch offen oder das Ergebnis wurde noch nicht verbucht" (position is still open or the result has not been booked yet), and precisely the most valuable part (planned risk vs. actual result) never appears. Direction of the fix: also write the ticket in the group path.
    - ⚠ **Minor follow-up defect:** a journal row with ticket `0` is still clickable in the dashboard and opens a file for "ticket 0" that lumps together all ticketless rows.
    - **Further limitations:** `CLOSE_MAN` does not count as a result (the file only knows `CLOSE`), the list is limited to 200 tickets, and the image caption converts the file name timestamp (server time) as if it were local time — the order is right, the displayed time may be shifted.
    - **Dependency:** the trade file stands and falls with the ticket trail in the journal and therefore inherits item 21 in full. It is an additional reason to measure item 21 before funded operation — but **no** substitute for the proof required there.

26. 🟡 **Multi-language support** *(v0.53–v0.61, partially wired)* — panel DE/EN via `InpLang`: `T()`/`TF()` with **153** keys, placeholders `{0}`…`{3}` instead of `%s`/`%d`, so that the order of the values stays free per language; an unknown key returns the key itself and is immediately noticeable in the panel instead of staying empty. Dashboard: `cockpit/i18n.js` contains **346** keys in **de/en/fa** — complete, no key is missing in any language; Persian runs via `dir="rtl"`.
    - ⚠ **The dashboard part is not fully wired:** `t()` is called exclusively from `applyLang()` for elements with `data-i18n` — and there are exactly **11** of those in `dashboard.html` (section headings). All dynamically generated content (lock monitor, buffer gauge, rule cards, statistics, calendar, journal, notifications) is hard-wired in German, as are the number and date formats (`de-DE`); `day.html`, `report.html` and `trade.html` have no translation at all. The switch therefore affects 11 of 346 texts. Either convert the renderers to `t()` or, until then, do not list the feature as finished.
    - **Deliberate and correct for the prop firm view:** journal entries stay German. The audit trail must not depend on which language was set in the browser on day X — in addition the cockpit server recognises the verdicts by text pattern (`Risiko ERHOEHT`, `net …`); a translated line would silently render the evaluation useless.

27. 🟡 **Cockpit display correctness: multi-account and lock monitor** *(v0.46/v0.48, built, untested)* — several accounts in the **same** terminal share one journal file. Without an account specification the server summed all accounts, and the numbers of the old account stuck to the new one. Now the dashboard, without an explicit choice, takes **the account running live** from `/state`; accounts that exist only in the journal appear as "nur Historie" (history only) in the switcher; and an old account finds its journal via its **own rows** (account column), not via the by-now foreign `cockpit.json`. The lock monitor shows cooldown, day lock, week lock, max loss and "Tagesziel erreicht" (daily target reached) with a countdown that keeps running in the browser between server queries (mm:ss or h:mm:ss).
    - **Honest classification:** the account separation is a **client** correction. Server side, `parseAll()` only filters when `?acct=` is passed along; a direct call to `/journal` or `/analytics` **without** `acct` still returns the sum of all accounts in the same file. All links inside the cockpit set the parameter — but "one account = one number" only holds for the path through the user interface, not for the interface itself.
    - ⚠ **The countdown assumes midnight and Sunday.** End of day and end of week are computed in the browser from server time as 00:00 and Sunday 24:00; `InpDayResetHour`/`InpWeekStartDay` are not in `mamal_cockpit.json` at all. If a deviating reset time or week boundary is set for a prop firm (item 13), the monitor shows the wrong remaining time. Pure **display** — enforcement still computes correctly in `SrvTime()` — but before using such a profile, either add the fields or suppress the countdown.

28. ✅ **Silent failure, second class: infinite recursion in the panel** *(v0.61, fixed, untested)* — a search-and-replace run over the German texts had applied the replacements **inside the translation table** as well; 22 entries thereby called `T()` from within `T()`. Consequence: stack overflow in the log, the EA died in the middle of drawing the panel, the panel stayed half empty. Fixed — the table no longer contains any `T()`/`TF()` call.
    - **Why this belongs in this list:** the crash does not only hit the display. `DrawPanel()` runs in the same `Cycle()` as enforcement, close evaluation and cockpit writing — an error in the drawing path takes the protection with it. After the master deadlock (item 16) this is the **second** way for enforcement to come to a halt without any error message appearing.
    - **Binding lesson:** a replacement run must never touch the translation table. Before every release: start the EA, let the panel draw completely, check the expert log for "stack overflow" — **and** run the cross-check from item 16 (do `CLOSE` lines still arrive after every close, does `mamal_cockpit.json` not age?).

## Honest limitations (always apply)

### New since v0.45 (applies in addition)

- **RISK FREE is a button, not an automatism.** The SL only moves when the trader presses it, only on the **chart symbol**, only for positions that are already in profit and far enough away from the price, and only for the tool's own positions. Everything else still has to be delivered by R7/R1.
- **The mandatory weekly risk is discipline, not a limit.** It forces a conscious decision before the panel acts. It limits neither the daily nor the total loss (R3/R4/R4b/R18 do that) and does not stop anyone from trading manually past the panel — only R22 bites there, with its known latency and compliance reservations.
- **Behavioural data is observation, not protection.** `SLTP_MOVE`, screenshots and candle thirds change no rule behaviour. They explain afterwards why a loss grew larger than planned — they did not prevent it. And they show the master chart, not necessarily the traded symbol.
- **The trade file is an evaluation of the journal, not an independent source.** If a line is missing — or if it lacks the ticket (item 25) — it is missing in the file as well. An empty or inconspicuous file entry proves nothing; the only dependable source remains the journal CSV, in case of doubt against the broker's account statement.
- **The language switch covers the dashboard only partially** (item 26). Anyone setting the interface to English or Persian will still get predominantly German texts. The journal deliberately stays German — that is the audit trail, not negligence.
- **Every error in the drawing path is a protection failure.** The panel and enforcement share `Cycle()`. A crash in the panel (v0.61: infinite recursion) stops enforcement just as silently as a missing master — and just as invisibly.


**As long as open ⬜ items exist: `Demo-only, not prop-firm-ready`.**
