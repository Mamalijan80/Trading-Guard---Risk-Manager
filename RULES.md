# TradingGuard — Rule Contract (versioned · first edition 2026-06-25 · valid for code state v0.35)

This is the **single source of truth**. EA inputs and cockpit config are derived from this table; if another document deviates, **this one** applies.

**"Frozen" means: not spontaneous, not intraday — not: immutable.** The contract has been amended several times since the first edition (extension v2, deactivations v0.20/v0.22, R7 rewrite v0.33, hardening v0.34, R22 v0.35). That is intentional and not a breach — the rule is:
- Every change is entered **here first**, with a **version stamp** and a justification, and only then implemented in code.
- Changes are **never made intraday** and **never under pressure** (no loosening after a loss) — the EA enforces this technically via `InpTightenOnly`/`Eff*` (loosening takes effect at the day rollover at the earliest).
- The **change history** is in the status block below and in the changelog of `docs/STATUS.md`. What is not listed there is not contract.

*Exempt from amendment are the deliberately frozen historical artifacts `docs/REVIEW-v0.14.md` and `docs/BUGHUNT-v0.17.md` — they describe their respective state and are not updated.*

**Profile:** default prop firm = **FTMO** (demo first), account 10k/20k/25k EUR · instruments XAUUSD, US30, EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY · scalping + intraday. **Goal: multi-prop-firm** — all limits are inputs; FTMO is only the default profile. > **How to read "Value (default)" — two conventions that apply to this entire file:**
> 1. **Auto-scale is on.** `InpAutoScale=true` is the shipped default. What is stated is therefore always the **effectively active** value (derived from risk/trade); the fixed percentage behind it is only the **fallback when `InpAutoScale=false`**. Affects R2, R3 and R12 — there the two numbers differ (derivation see the "Auto-scale" section below).
> 2. **All EUR amounts are 20k examples.** The profile allows 10k/20k/25k; the binding figure is always the **percentage**. The EUR numbers in this and all derived documents are converted to the **EUR 20,000 account base** and serve illustration only — on 10k they halve, on 25k they are 25 % higher. The EA calculates exclusively in percent against the real account base (`InpInitialBalance` or the daily/weekly anchors), never against a hard-wired EUR sum.

**v0.35 = R22 "panel trades only".** New rule, default **on** (`InpCloseManualTrades=true`). Every order with magic 0 is queued and closed or — as a pending — deleted; orders with a foreign magic ≠ 0 are left untouched. Contract-relevant:
- **Justification:** only the panel path (`DoEntry`) passes through the entry gates — AutoTrading, lock, unsafe base, R4b warn gate, R13 daily target, R5 cooldown, R16 session/news, R25 revenge, R14 minimum pause, R7 SL line/side, R15 broker `STOPLEVEL` + min SL, R1 lot calculation, R2 idea cap, R3 daily budget, R12 heat, R17 correlation. A manual click in the terminal bypasses them **all**. Previously such a trade was, with an active lock under `WatchScope=TOOL_ONLY` (default, enforced in FundedMode), not even closed, only warned about at most 1×/minute.
- **Account-wide, scope-independent:** R22 compares the magic directly and is therefore the **only** enforcement function that bypasses the `InScope()` filter — no symbol filter, and active in FundedMode as well (see "Honest limits" item 6).
- **No grandfathering at startup:** `OnInit` counts pre-existing magic-0 orders and reports them (journal event `INFO`), but closes nothing itself — the close only happens once the instance is a **confirmed master** (≥2 cycles), and not at all on a passive instance.
- **Interaction with the loss rules:** a trade closed by R22 is out of scope for `ResolveHistory()` and `ReconcileDayRisk` → **no** contribution to R5 cooldown, R6 losing streak, R19 de-risk, R25 revenge and **no** consumption of the R3 daily budget. The realized loss does, however, fully hit the equity-based limits: **R4, R4b, R18**. R22 does not count as trader fault in the sense of the losing-streak count (only R7/R1/R8 do there).
- **Cockpit:** with `InpCloseManualTrades=false`, R22 appears in the dashboard as a "— aus" (off) chip. In "Deine Schwächen" (your weaknesses), every journal line with event `CLOSE` is counted — that is, every retry, every FINAL-FAIL and every discard as well. **The R22 counter can count a single manual trade multiple times**; it is a tendency, not a trade count. The `INFO` entry from `OnInit` is not counted. R22 does not appear in the on-chart panel.
- **Known edge:** the distinction from foreign EAs hangs solely on `Magic != 0`. Panel trades are sent with `InpMagic` (default `990201`) — **whoever sets `InpMagic=0` lets R22 clear away their own panel trades.** The code does not protect against this. `InpMagic` therefore stays ≠ 0.

**v0.45 = R22 context: manual trades are closed — and are now visible.**

**Nothing** changes about R22 itself — the rule closes magic-0 orders unchanged as described above. What is new is that such a trade no longer disappears without a trace: `ResolveManualHistory()` journals every closed position that is **not in the watch scope** and is **not a registered panel ticket** (`RGOPN_<ticket>`) as its own event **`CLOSE_MAN`**. This covers three cases: the order R22 just reverted itself; every manual trade when `InpCloseManualTrades=false` or the EA/master was not running at the time; and every trade of a foreign EA (magic ≠ 0 and ≠ `InpMagic`) that R22 fundamentally does not touch.
- **Display, not judgment.** The cockpit marks these lines as **"MANUELL"** (manual) (orange row in the event list and on the day page, ✋ in the calendar, its own tile "Davon manuell" (of which manual), its own column in the weekly report). The journal tag reads explicitly "Manueller/fremder Trade (Magic x) — ausserhalb der Tool-Regeln, nur Anzeige" (manual/foreign trade (magic x) — outside the tool rules, display only).
- **Rule effect stays exactly as defined in the v0.35 block:** `CLOSE_MAN` produces **no** losing streak (R5/R6), **no** de-risk step (R19), **no** revenge window (R25), **no** consumption of the R3 daily budget and **no** contribution to R2/R12/R17. The realized loss comes through only via equity — that is, on **R4, R4b, R18**.
- **Two journal lines, two purposes, no double money.** The R22 close writes a `CLOSE` line with the reason "Queue OK (…): R22 Manueller Trade …" (R22 manual trade) **without** a net figure; the realized result is carried exclusively by the `CLOSE_MAN` line. The cockpit sums net from `CLOSE` lines **with** a net figure **plus** `CLOSE_MAN` — so the R22 line drops out of the money total. Conversely it remains the line that counts as R22 in "Deine Schwächen": the EA column `RuleId` is empty on queue lines, the cockpit server derives `R22` from the tag — which is why **every retry and every FINAL-FAIL** still counts (see v0.35 block, "Cockpit").
- **Dedup (v0.44/v0.45, this was a genuine bug):** the `RGP_` markers of the rule path used to be pruned **immediately** with the history floor — the ticket fallback then evaluated the same position again. A **grace period of 6 h** now applies. In addition, **both** paths set a shared `RGM_` marker ("already shown in the cockpit"), so that a trade never lands in the net figure as `CLOSE` **and** `CLOSE_MAN` when the scope or magic changes.
- **Window:** what is reported is **today's server day**, what is aggregated is **7 days backwards** — a partial close from yesterday belongs to the same position net as the remainder closed today. As long as a remainder is still open, nothing is reported at all. If setting the `RGM_` marker fails, the report is omitted **entirely** (one-off `INFO` line) — better invisible than a journal flood every 2 s.
- **Discipline streak:** a day with manual trades **interrupts** the counter "Tage ohne Regel-Block" (days without a rule block). Previously the streak kept running if someone traded exclusively around the tool all day (no `OPEN`, no block) — that was a flattered metric.
- *(Doc correction on the "known edge" above: the sentence "the code does not protect against this" describes v0.35. Since **v0.40**, `OnInit` corrects an invalid `InpMagic<=0` to `990201` itself and reports this loudly; in addition, `EnforceManualTrades()` bails out when `InpMagic==0`. The distinction still hangs solely on the magic — but the self-clearing trap is closed.)*

## Definitions
- **Idea key** = `symbol + direction` (e.g. `XAUUSD|BUY`). Implemented identically in the EA and the cockpit.
- **Risk of a position** = `|Entry − SL| × TickValue × Lots`, as a % of equity.
- **Day start equity** = captured at server midnight (`SrvTime()` = PC clock + server offset maintained on each tick; MQL4 has no `TimeTradeServer`) — never blindly trust stored values (hardening: derive from broker history).
- **SL/TP deadline (R7):** a position without SL **or** TP is "naked". From the moment the EA sees it naked (opening without SL/TP **or** later removal), you have **4 s** (in the news blackout **0 s**), otherwise the EA closes it. **The anchor is the SL/TP removal, not `OrderOpenTime`** — otherwise an old trade would effectively have 0 s after the SL is removed. **Tool trades are treated identically** (no immediate-close special case any more) — it applies uniformly to every position in the watch scope (default `TOOL_ONLY` = panel trades). *(Doc correction v0.33: the contract named 5 s from opening + immediate close for tool trades; the code does 4 s from removal for all in-scope positions.)*
  - **Hardening (v0.34):** the grace clock lies **persistently per ticket** in GlobalVariables (`RG_NK_<ticket>`) — it survives **terminal restart, recompile, timeframe change and master handoff**; previously each of these cases granted a fresh deadline. And it is **not** deleted immediately when SL/TP are set again, but only after a **healing period of 60 s** (`RG_NKH_<ticket>`); if the position becomes naked again before that, the healing period lapses and the old clock keeps running. This removes the trick of "tapping the SL briefly to reset the clock".
  - **Tighten-only (v0.34):** the SL/TP obligation itself (`InpRequireSL`/`InpRequireTP`), once active, **can no longer be switched off intraday** (`EffRequireSL()`/`EffRequireTP()`); a loosening only takes effect at the day rollover — the same self-lock logic as with the loss limits (R10).
- **Panel trade / manual trade / foreign EA (R22):** the distinction is made exclusively via the **magic number** of the order.
  - **Panel trade** = sent by the EA via the one-click entry, magic = `InpMagic` (default `990201`). Only this path runs through the entry gates (R1/R2/R3/R5/R7/R12/R13/R14/R15/R16/R17/R25 + locks).
  - **Manual trade** = **magic 0** — opened by hand in the terminal (market execution or pending), bypassing the EA. Exactly these orders are what R22 reverts.
  - **Foreign EA** = magic ≠ 0 **and** ≠ `InpMagic`. Is **not** touched by R22.
  - This classification is independent of the **watch scope**: `InScope()` (default `TOOL_ONLY`) decides what the remaining rules monitor; R22 checks the magic directly and therefore also takes effect where the scope sees nothing. Consequence: with R22 active, `WatchScope=TOOL_PLUS_MANUAL` is practically ineffective — magic-0 orders then exist for seconds at most anyway (startup hint in the EA).
- **Relationship R3↔R4:** R3 (**2.0 %**) = max. *planned* risk deployed; R4 (2 %) = hard daily emergency brake on an equity basis. *(Doc correction: 3 % was stated here — R3 has been 2.0 % = 400 € since v0.20, see the rules table.)*

## Gate: weekly risk obligation (R23, v0.49–v0.51)

Up to v0.48 the risk chooser was a convenience: whoever chose nothing traded silently with `InpRiskPerTradePct`. Since **v0.49** the weekly decision is a **gate** — without a defined weekly risk there is no trading (`InpRequireWeeklyRisk=true`, default on). The gate depends on `InpRiskChooser=true`: without the chooser there is no input field and therefore no obligation either.

- **Where it takes effect:** in `DoEntry` as the **third** check — after AutoTrading, lock and "base unsafe", **before** the R4b warn gate, R13 daily target, R5 cooldown, R16, R25 and R14. Rejection comes with a panel message and a journal line `BLOCKED`, tag "Wochen-Risiko nicht festgelegt" (weekly risk not defined).
- **What the panel shows:** status pill **"RISIKO FESTLEGEN"** (set risk) (EN "SET RISK", blue), **BUY/SELL greyed out**, blue highlighted frame around the input field. Until confirmation, the value in the field is only the input default — **a displayed number is not yet a decision**.
- **Operation (v0.49–v0.51):** input field (`OBJ_EDIT`) + button **SETZEN** (set); **Enter** in the field confirms just the same (`CHARTEVENT_OBJECT_ENDEDIT`). "0,25", "0.25" and "0,25 %" are read identically — without the comma conversion MQL4 would read "0,25" as 0 and silently discard the input. Values ≤ 0 or > 1.0 are rejected and the field is reset to the valid state; accepted values are clamped to `InpRiskStep` … `InpRiskMaxPct`, hard-capped at **1.0 %**. The field text is only written when the valid value changes — otherwise the panel redraw (2×/s) would overwrite the typing in progress.
- **Weekly binding (semantics unchanged since v0.36):** the **first** choice of the week is free and takes effect **immediately** (it additionally sets `RG_EFF_RISK`, otherwise the daily tighten-only layer would push it back down). **Every** later change — including a **reduction** — is only queued for the next week; whoever types the currently fixed value again cancels a queued change.
- **Effect beyond R1:** the weekly value is the basis of `EffRiskPct()` and therefore of the entire auto-scale cascade — idea cap (R2), daily budget (R3), portfolio heat (R12) and the correlation cap coupled to it (R17) are derived from it.
- **When it asks again (v0.62):** what is checked is whether a confirmation exists for the **current** week (`RG_WEEK_RISK` > 0 **and** `RG_WEEK_RISK_IDX` = current week index). `RG_WEEK_RISK_IDX` is set **exclusively** by a genuine confirmation from the trader (`SetWeekRisk`). The weekly roll does **not** touch it — which makes every new week automatically unconfirmed and the gate ask again. So who has to set it anew: **everyone, every week**, plus whoever has never chosen, whose `RG_*` GlobalVariables have been deleted or expired, and whoever has **changed account** (`WipeAllState()` deletes the entire `RG` namespace).
  - **Up to v0.61 this was a hole:** the roll set `RG_WEEK_RISK_IDX` to the new week as well — the obligation effectively took hold **exactly once**, at the very first start. Every Monday after that, the panel stood silently on "AKTIV" (active).
  - **The value carries over, the confirmation does not.** `RG_WEEK_RISK`/`RG_EFF_RISK` keep the last confirmed value. That is not convenience but necessity: if the effective risk fell back to the input default, **R1 would force-close every weekend position on Monday** that was correctly sized according to the confirmed weekly value — and that loss close would count as trader fault in the streak and cooldown.
  - **A queued change is not activated by the roll.** It is a proposal, not an instruction: on Monday it only pre-fills the input field and only takes effect via **SETZEN** (set). Otherwise a queued reduction could trigger the same forced closing, and a queued increase would loosen the entire auto-scale cascade in a week that was never confirmed.
  - **No instance overrides another:** whether the obligation applies is decided by each instance via its **own** inputs (`WeekRiskPending()`). The roll only runs on the master — if the gate depended on the master's inputs, a single chart with the chooser switched off would lift the obligation for **all** charts.
- **Fail-closed, but no substitute for a lock:** the state lies exclusively in GlobalVariables, **not** in the signed lockstate file. Whoever deletes them loosens nothing — they land in the block. Conversely, R23 is a **pure entry gate**: already open positions are not touched, there is no retroactive enforcement as with R5/R13/R16/R25.
- **Cockpit:** the rejection carries **no RuleId** — the EA does not write one (the tag does not start with "Rxx") and the server does not derive one from the text either. It therefore appears in the event list, in the "Abgelehnt" (rejected) tile and in the discipline ratio, but **not** in "Deine Schwächen" (your weaknesses) under an R number — and like every block it **interrupts** the streak "Tage ohne Regel-Block" (days without a rule block).

## Control element: the four close buttons (v0.40)

Below BUY/SELL, four buttons sit in the on-chart panel. They are **operation, not a rule**: no R number, no input to switch them off, no tighten-only reservation — they can only **lower** the risk level.

| Button | Effect |
|--------|---------|
| **FULL CLOSE** | closes all open positions of the account |
| **CHART CLOSE** | closes all open positions in the symbol of the chart that was clicked |
| **FULL 50 %** | halves every open position of the account |
| **CHART 50 %** | halves every open position in the symbol of this chart |

- **Reach deliberately account-wide, including foreign magics** (deliberate user decision) — the buttons ignore the watch scope, like R22. **Pending orders remain untouched**: only `OP_BUY`/`OP_SELL` are touched.
- **No gate, no query, no confirmation.** The click closes immediately and also runs **during a day lock (R4), total lock (R4b) and cooldown (R5)** as well as on a **passive instance** (master status is not checked) — risk reduction must never be blocked. The only brake is `IsTradeContextBusy()`: then nothing happens and the panel reports "Trade-Kontext belegt — bitte gleich nochmal klicken." (trade context busy — please click again shortly).
- **50 % rounds down in favor of the remainder** (to `MODE_LOTSTEP`, with an FP epsilon — otherwise 0.06 lot yields a 0.02 instead of a 0.03 half). If the half **or** the remaining rest is below the min lot, the position stays **unchanged** (message "… zu klein (Min-Lot)" (… too small (min lot))).
- **No loss laundering:** a trade closed via a button does **not** get a `MarkEaClosed`. For panel trades this means: the loss counts normally in the losing streak (R5/R6), de-risk (R19) and revenge window (R25). The panic button is no way out of the streak. The P/L of foreign magics stays, as always, outside the rule evaluation — but it does appear as `CLOSE_MAN` in the net figure.
- **Ticket registry:** on a 50 % close the remainder gets a **new ticket**. The EA registers it (`RGOPN_<ticket>`) — preferably via the MT4 comment `from #<ticket>`, alternatively only on an **exact** match of open time, price, magic and remaining lots. Otherwise close detection would lose the remainder. The target tickets are also **frozen before** the first close, so that a freshly created remainder is not halved again in the same pass.
- **Journal:** every ticket produces a line `PANEL_CLOSE` — on success with "Voll/50 % · Konto/Chart" (full/50 % · account/chart), on failure with an error code. The **live event list** of the dashboard filters on `OPEN`/`CLOSE`/`CLOSE_MAN`/`BLOCKED`; `PANEL_CLOSE` does **not** appear there, but it does on the day page and in the journal CSV.

## Control element: RISK FREE — SL to break-even (v0.46)

Below the four close buttons sits a wide button **"RISK FREE — SL AUF BREAK-EVEN"** (risk free — SL to break-even) (`InpBreakEvenBtn`, default on). Like the close buttons it is **operation, not a rule**: no R number, no tighten-only reservation — it can only **lower** the risk level.

- **Reach: only the symbol of the clicked chart.** The button calls the break-even run fixed in **chart mode**; positions in other symbols remain untouched — the label does not say so. In addition, the **watch scope** applies: only what `InScope()` covers (default `TOOL_ONLY` = panel trades) **or** what is registered as a panel ticket (`RGOPN_<ticket>`, e.g. the remainder after a 50 % close) is touched. **Foreign magics are deliberately left untouched** — their EA logic may rely on its own SL; whoever sets `WatchScope=ALL_POSITIONS` lifts exactly this protection. **Pending orders remain untouched** (only `OP_BUY`/`OP_SELL`).
- **Break-even is `OrderOpenPrice()`**, plus `InpBreakEvenBufferPts` points in the trade direction (default **0**). MT4 opens a BUY at the ask and closes at the bid (SELL the other way round) — the **spread is already contained in the entry price**, which is why the entry is the correct break-even. The buffer is intended for the **commission**, not for the spread.
- **Only positions in profit.** Measurement is against the closing price (BUY: bid, SELL: ask). If it is not yet beyond the target, the position stays unchanged (message "… noch nicht im Gewinn/zu nah" (… not yet in profit/too close)).
- **An SL is never loosened.** If it is already at or beyond the target, nothing happens. In addition, `MODE_STOPLEVEL` is checked: if the distance price↔target falls below the broker minimum distance, it is skipped instead of letting an `OrderModify` run into error 130.
- **No gate, no query, no confirmation.** The click takes effect immediately and also runs **during a day lock (R4), total lock (R4b) and cooldown (R5)** as well as on a **passive instance** (master status is not checked) — risk reduction must never be blocked. The only brake is `IsTradeContextBusy()`. The button does **not** have its own AutoTrading check: if AutoTrading is off, the `OrderModify` fails and the line stands as "FEHLGESCHLAGEN err=…" (failed err=…) in the journal.
- **Journal:** one line `BREAKEVEN` per ticket — on success "SL auf Break-Even gezogen (#Ticket)" (SL pulled to break-even (#ticket)), on failure with an error code. The **live event list** of the dashboard does **not** include it, just like `PANEL_CLOSE`; in the **trade file** (v0.62) it appears as the sentence "Position wurde per RISK-FREE auf Break-Even abgesichert." (position was secured to break-even via RISK-FREE).
- **Second track, intentional:** the behavior tracking (v0.47) does not distinguish **who** moved the SL. A RISK-FREE move therefore additionally produces an `SLTP_MOVE` line with the verdict "Risiko gesenkt, Break-Even erreicht (vorteilhaft)" (risk lowered, break-even reached (advantageous)) together with a `slmove` screenshot — so in the trade file the click counts as a move as well.

## Honest limits (always apply)
1. Client-side = **detect-and-revert**, not prevent: a forbidden click fills in ~100–800 ms and is then closed (slippage as the price).
2. "No override" is locally only **friction** — genuine immutability only on a locked VPS + the FTMO server limit.
3. Mac sleep stops the EA = the floor is offline; overnight only via VPS/firm limit.
4. **R22 does not prevent the manual trade — it reverts it.** The order is executed, it is in the market, and the EA closes it afterwards at bid/ask (pendings via `OrderDelete`). **The trader bears spread and slippage**; with a gap this can turn into a real loss. R22 is a discipline rule, not an execution protection.
5. **Detection latency of R22, honestly:** the blanket "~100–800 ms" from item 1 is **not substantiated** for R22. Derivable from the code: clock source timer 1 s or tick (min. 500 ms apart), plus close throttle 300 ms, plus possibly up to 2 s modify-quiet window (hard-capped at a 10 s series — so whoever constantly drags SL/TP delays R22), plus retry/backoff on errors. Realistically: **fractions of a second to several seconds**; with a closed market (error 132) up to **15 min** per attempt. R22 also does **not take effect at all** if the EA is not running, AutoTrading is off, the instance is not a confirmed master, or the timer does not start. If all close attempts fail, the ticket drops out of the queue with FINAL-FAIL and the order **stays open** — it is queued again in the next pass.
6. **R22 also works in FundedMode** and thereby closes positions the EA did not open itself — the only place where the `InScope()` safety design is deviated from. Whether a prop firm accepts that an EA closes foreign/manual orders on the challenge account is **firm-specific and must be verified before going live** (the FTMO key figures above are an assumed state). Whoever does not want this: `InpCloseManualTrades=false`.

## What is NOT protected by rules

The contract above describes what the EA enforces. Equally important is the counter-list — what ends up in the account without any rule taking hold. Since v0.44/v0.45 this gap is at least **visible** (`CLOSE_MAN`, "MANUELL" (manual)); that does not mean it is closed.

1. **Manual and foreign trades count towards the money, not towards the rules.** Every position outside the watch scope (default `TOOL_ONLY`) — phone order, terminal click, foreign EA — brings its realized result **fully** into the account and into the cockpit net figure. It is evaluated by **no** rule: no losing streak (R5/R6), no cooldown, no revenge window (R25), no de-risk ladder (R19), no consumption of the R3 daily budget, no contribution to R2 idea cap, R12 portfolio heat and R17 correlation (the risk sums skip out-of-scope positions), and no SL/TP obligation (R7). A manual trade without an SL stays without an SL.
2. **What does take hold anyway is only the emergency brake.** The equity-based lines **R4, R4b, R18** measure account equity and see every loss, no matter where it comes from. That is the only protection that reaches manual trades — and it only takes hold once the damage is already done. That is not risk management.
3. **R22 reverts, it does not prevent** (see "Honest limits" 4 and 5): the order fills for real, and the trader bears spread, slippage and any gap. **Foreign EAs (magic ≠ 0 and ≠ `InpMagic`) are fundamentally not touched by R22** — for them there is no revert mechanism at all, only the `CLOSE_MAN` display.
4. **Without running enforcement there is no rule at all — and you cannot tell from the panel.** Panel and display run on every instance; enforcement, close evaluation and cockpit writes are done **only by the confirmed master instance** (≥ 2 cycles). Up to **v0.42**, this election could fail **permanently**: the deinit release **deleted** `RG_MASTER`, and `GlobalVariableSetOnCondition` cannot create a missing variable — after removing a master chart, a master election was never possible again, while the panel kept running unchanged. Fixed (release via `Set(...,0)`, a missing slot is created via `GlobalVariableTemp`, loud alarm + `PROTECT_OFF` journal entry after 30 s without a master). The lesson stands: **EA off, AutoTrading off, terminal closed, instance not master = no protection** — and apart from the 30 s alarm, nobody speaks up.

What is NOT protected by rules — item 5 (replaced)

5. **Close buttons and RISK FREE are manual work, not protection.** They only take hold when someone clicks. There is neither an automatic flat mechanism nor an automatic break-even move that triggers in their place. **RISK FREE** additionally only works in the **symbol of the clicked chart** and only on positions in the watch scope or registered panel tickets: a manual or foreign trade is **not** secured by it, and neither is a position in a neighboring symbol — and after the click, nothing trails the SL further.

6. **The zero-config base (v0.38) is no substitute for verification.** The challenge base is derived from the deposit history (balance entry `OrderType 6`, first deposit), the day base is reconstructed via `ReconstructedBalanceAt(ServerDayStart())`; an implausible amount (< 10 % of the balance, e.g. a fee refund with a filtered history tab) is discarded and falls back to the current balance. "BASIS UNSICHER" (base unsafe) is thus practically abolished, `InpInitialBalance`/`InpDayStartBase` are now only an **optional override**, fail-closed remains only with `g_initialBalance<=0` (no broker handshake). Precisely for that reason: **if the history is incomplete, the base is guessed, not reported as wrong.** Check the displayed base once against the account statement before going live.
7. **Prop firm rules beyond the loss limits are not checked by the EA.** Consistency rules, minimum trading days, news and weekend requirements, payout logic: there is no rule in the contract for these (R21 payout protection, R26 trade count cap, R27 weekend flat are under "Open/proposals"; R16 session/news is available but **default OFF**). The challenge tracker in the dashboard counts trading days and extrapolates — **it enforces nothing.**

What is NOT protected by rules — new item 8

8. **The weekly risk obligation (R23) secures the decision, not the account.** It sits exclusively in the panel path `DoEntry`. An order triggered by hand in the terminal comes into existence even without a defined weekly risk — it then falls under R22 or under item 1 of this list. Even within the tool the gate is **one-off**: it checks **that** a value exists for the current week, not **which** one; and once the week is set, the gate does **not** ask again at the week change, as long as a master instance performs the roll and the `RG_*` GlobalVariables are in place (a new decision is only required after an account change, loss of the variables, or if no master ever ran). Moreover, a defined risk is not yet an observed one: the number is enforced only by R1 (lot), R2, R3 and R12.
